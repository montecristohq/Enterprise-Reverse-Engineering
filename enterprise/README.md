# Enterprise Reverse Engineering Framework (EREF)

## Full Specification — 7 Volumes + Enterprise Reconstruction

EREF is a structured methodology for disassembling an enterprise, understanding each subsystem independently, tracing the flows and dependencies between them, and reconstructing the enterprise as one integrated machine.

> **Disassemble. Understand. Reconstruct.**

## Full Architecture

1. **Volume I — Institutional Anatomy**  
   Corporate, ownership, governance, legal, regulatory.

2. **Volume II — Market, Customer & Commercial Anatomy**  
   Market structure, customers, jobs-to-be-done, acquisition, sales, pricing, distribution, retention.

3. **Volume III — Product, Service & Value-Flow Anatomy**  
   Products, services, product dependencies, customer journeys, money/material/data/value flows.

4. **Volume IV — Operating-System Anatomy**  
   Operations, procurement, supply chain, manufacturing/service delivery, logistics, workforce, quality, bottlenecks, resilience.

5. **Volume V — Technology, Data & Infrastructure Anatomy**  
   Core systems, architecture, data, integration, AI, security, reliability, technical debt, build-vs-buy.

6. **Volume VI — Economic & Capital Anatomy**  
   Revenue, costs, unit economics, financial statements, cash flow, capital, ROIC, scenarios, sensitivities.

7. **Volume VII — Strategic Anatomy**  
   Management, culture, incentives, competition, moat, risk, flywheels, bottlenecks, optionality, future evolution.

8. **Final Stage — Enterprise Reconstruction**  
   Recombine Volumes I–VII into one coherent enterprise model.

## EREF Compact vs Full

- **EREF Compact:** 5 volumes; useful for faster or smaller-company research.
- **EREF Full:** 7 volumes; preferred for institutional-grade teardown and complex enterprises.

## Universal Questions

For every material component ask:

- **What?**
- **How?**
- **Who?**
- **Where?**
- **When?**
- **Why?**

Then analyze:

- **Structure**
- **Flow**
- **Control**
- **Economics**
- **Risk**

## Non-Negotiable Depth Rule

Depth follows the subject, not page count.

If a topic requires 3 pages, write 3 pages. If it requires 30 pages, write 30 pages. Never compress later sections because earlier sections became long.

> **Completeness before concision.**

## Evidence Labels

Use:

- **CONFIRMED FACT**
- **COMPANY CLAIM**
- **THIRD-PARTY ESTIMATE**
- **ANALYTICAL INFERENCE**
- **HYPOTHESIS**
- **UNKNOWN**

## EREF + TREF

EREF studies the enterprise. TREF studies a technology.

A critical system identified in **EREF Volume V** can be handed to TREF for a separate technical teardown.
